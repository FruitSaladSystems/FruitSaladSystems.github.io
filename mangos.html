Features

Everything it does, it does on purpose.

No preinstalled clutter, no background processes you didn't approve. Just the parts of an OS that earn their place.

01

Instant boot

Go from cold start to desktop in under four seconds, every time — no progress bar to stare at.

02

Adaptive workspaces

Windows and apps rearrange themselves around what you're doing, not the other way around.

03

Built-in privacy shield

Every app runs sandboxed by default. Nothing phones home without you knowing about it first.

04

One command, everything

A universal search bar that opens apps, finds files, and runs system commands from one place.
